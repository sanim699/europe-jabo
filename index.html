Visa basics, in short

Every country listed here requires a national (type D) student visa for Bangladeshi citizens, applied for at the respective embassy in Dhaka. Each country guide breaks down the exact document checklist, but expect to need: an acceptance letter, proof of funds, accommodation proof, health insurance, and academic transcripts.
