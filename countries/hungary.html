Studying in Hungary

Hungary is one of the most popular EU destinations for Bangladeshi students, largely thanks to the Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship, which covers tuition and living costs for successful applicants.

Tuition & scholarships

Without a scholarship, tuition at Hungarian public universities for international students typically runs a few thousand euros per year depending on the program. The Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship, funded by the Hungarian government, covers full tuition, a monthly stipend, and health insurance for accepted students — it's highly competitive and applications usually open in the final months of the year for the following academic year, so plan well ahead.

Visa document checklist

  • University acceptance/enrollment letter (or scholarship award letter)
  • Valid passport (6+ months beyond intended stay)
  • Proof of financial means (bank statement or scholarship confirmation)
  • Proof of accommodation in Hungary
  • Health insurance valid for Hungary/Schengen area
  • Academic transcripts and certificates (translated/attested if required)
  • Completed visa application + appointment at the Hungarian embassy in Dhaka (or nearest accredited consulate)

Cost of living

Budapest is noticeably cheaper than most Western European capitals. A reasonable monthly budget covering rent, food, and local transport is roughly €500-€750/month for Budapest, and somewhat lower in smaller university towns like Debrecen or Pécs. Stipendium Hungaricum recipients get a fixed monthly stipend that covers a significant part of this.

Application timeline

  • Sep-Nov: Stipendium Hungaricum portal usually opens — this is your priority deadline to plan around
  • Dec-Jan: Stipendium Hungaricum application deadline (varies slightly year to year — check the official portal directly)
  • Feb-Apr: University-specific (non-scholarship) applications often still open in parallel
  • Apr-Jun: Scholarship results announced; direct university admission decisions also arrive around this window
  • Jun-Aug: Visa document preparation and embassy appointment booking
  • Sep: Semester begins

Language & exam requirements

Most English-taught programs in Hungary ask for IELTS 6.0-6.5 or equivalent, though exact thresholds vary by university and program level. Some universities and Stipendium Hungaricum applications also involve an online or in-person interview as part of the selection process — this is common even at bachelor's level, so prepare to discuss your motivation and background clearly.

Part-time work & staying after graduation

International students in Hungary are generally allowed to work part-time during their studies, with hour limits set by their residence permit conditions. After graduation, there are pathways to apply for a residence permit tied to employment if you secure a job offer — but rules and hour limits can change, so always verify current conditions with the immigration office or embassy before making plans.

This guide shares general information only. Always confirm current requirements with the Hungarian embassy in Dhaka or your target university before applying.
